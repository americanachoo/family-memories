Family Memoir Voice Recorder

Welcome to your family memoir project! This tool helps you record your life memories by answering simple questions. You can:

  • Select a question category from the menu
  • Press "Record" and share your memories
  • Listen to your recordings
  • Email your recordings to save and share them
  • Return anytime to add more memories

Your recordings are saved on your device.

How to Set Up EmailJS for This App:

  1. Go to EmailJS.com and create a free account
  2. Add a new email service (like Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
  3. Create a new template with these parameters:
    • {{to_email}} - For recipient address
    • {{subject}} - For email subject
    • {{message}} - For message text
    • {{category}} - For question category
    • {{question}} - For the question text
    • {{date}} - For recording date
    • {{audio_data}} - For the audio attachment
  4. For the audio attachment, add this to your template body:
    5. <!-- Audio attachment --> <a href="{{audio_data}}" download="family_memoir_recording.wav">Download Audio Recording</a>
  5. Copy your Service ID, Template ID, and Public Key from the EmailJS dashboard

These settings will be saved on this device. Learn more about EmailJS (free for up to 200 emails/month).
