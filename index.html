Family Memoir Voice Recorder

Welcome to your family memoir project! This tool helps you record your life memories by answering simple questions. You can:

  • Select a question category from the menu
  • Press "Record" and share your memories
  • Listen to your recordings
  • Email your recordings to save and share them
  • Return anytime to add more memories

Your recordings are saved on your device.
